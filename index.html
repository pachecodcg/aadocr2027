Post-event report · IADR/AADOCR General Session 2026

Our first IADR/AADOCR booth put DCG at the front door of dental research.

From March 25 to 28, 2026, the Dental College of Georgia at Augusta University exhibited at the IADR/AADOCR General Session in San Diego for the first time. A delegation of 11 faculty members and five students staffed Booth #205, presented eight research works and saw a DCG student win the IADR Hatton Award and a DCG faculty member receive the AADOCR Anne D. Haffajee Fellowship.

01 · Outcomes

The booth returned four things a job posting cannot.

Booth #205 sat at the entry of the San Diego Convention Center exhibition hall, across from Colgate. Every attendee who walked onto the floor passed DCG first.

1

Recruitment in person

Attendees scanned the QR codes on our flyers and monitor to ask about and apply for open faculty positions, the booth's primary objective.

2

Visibility at scale

The putting green drew more traffic than the Colgate booth across the aisle, and several schools told us they planned to copy the idea.

3

A stage for our wins

The monitor announced the Hatton Award and the Haffajee Fellowship as they happened, in front of the whole research community.

4

Students who stay in research

Five students presented, staffed the booth and met the field. That exposure is hard to give them any other way.

06 · Recommendation

Renewing the membership keeps the booth for 2027.

The President's Circle institutional membership included the complimentary 2026 booth. The 2027 meeting is in Minneapolis in March. Four decisions would carry this year's results forward.

  1. 1
    Renew the President's Circle institutional membership

    Through the Dean's office, as in past years, so the complimentary 2027 booth is reserved.

  2. 2
    Set aside $3,750 per booth event

    Covering giveaways, electrical service, shipping and supplies, with the same model reused at the ADEA Annual Session.

  3. 3
    Name a logistics coordinator

    Mary Hood made the 2026 booth possible; the role should be formal, with the same scope.

  4. 4
    Start planning in November

    Right after the abstract deadline, so giveaways, shipping and staffing are settled months ahead.
